Introducing Our New Wide-Format Press!

By Sammy Worx Team September 28, 2025 News

We are thrilled to announce a major upgrade to the Sammy Worx workshop! We have officially installed the state-of-the-art **Apex Pro Wide-Format Press**, significantly boosting our capacity and quality for all large-scale printing projects, including banners, vehicle wraps, and custom signage.

This investment reflects our continued commitment to providing our clients with the best and fastest print solutions in the industry. The Apex Pro utilizes cutting-edge UV-LED curing technology, allowing us to print directly onto a vast array of rigid and flexible materials with stunning detail and vibrant, long-lasting color.

What This Means for Our Clients

The new press brings several immediate and measurable benefits to your projects:

  • **Faster Turnaround Times:** Increased printing speed means we can deliver large projects, such as event banners and hoarding signage, much quicker than before.
  • **Unrivaled Quality:** The machine offers higher resolution printing, making even large graphics appear crisp and detailed, eliminating banding and color inconsistencies.
  • **Wider Material Range:** We can now easily print on thicker, more diverse substrates, including glass, wood, metals, and specialized vinyls, expanding your creative possibilities.

Perfect for Large-Scale Branding

If your business relies on impactful, large-format graphics—whether for trade shows, retail window displays, or entire fleet vehicle branding—this new machine is game-changing. We can produce everything from custom wallpaper and floor graphics to massive outdoor billboards, all under one roof with guaranteed **"Best in the game" quality**.

We invite all our partners to visit the workshop soon and see the new Apex Pro in action! We believe in transparency and want you to be confident that your biggest branding assets are handled by the best technology available.

**Key Takeaway:** Our new wide-format press ensures faster production and higher quality for all large projects, from vehicle wraps to custom rigid signage. We're ready for your biggest ideas!
