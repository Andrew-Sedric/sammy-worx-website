5 Essential Design Tips for Non-Designers

By Jane Smith October 10, 2023 Design

Creating effective marketing materials involves more than just having a logo; it requires strategic design. If you're preparing files yourself before sending them to the printer, follow these five essential tips to ensure your final product looks professional and avoids common printing pitfalls.

1. Understand Resolution and Bleed

**Resolution** is key to crisp prints. All images and final files must be at least **300 DPI (Dots Per Inch)**. Anything lower will look pixelated when printed. **Bleed** is the area extending past the trim line—it prevents white edges if the paper shifts slightly during cutting. Always design your file with a minimum **3mm bleed** around all edges.

2. Master Color Modes: CMYK vs. RGB

This is the most frequent mistake! Screens use **RGB (Red, Green, Blue)** light, which has a huge color gamut. Printers use **CMYK (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Black)** ink. If you design in RGB, the final print will look muted or different because the printer can't match those bright screen colors. **Always set your file's color profile to CMYK** before beginning your design.

3. Prioritize Visual Hierarchy

What is the single most important piece of information? Make that **BIG**. The next most important? Make it slightly smaller. Use size, **bolding**, and contrast to guide the reader's eye naturally from the headline to the contact information. If everything is the same size, nothing stands out.

4. Choose Fonts Wisely (Limit to Two)

A successful design uses typefaces that are both readable and reflect the brand's personality. The golden rule: **use no more than two distinct fonts**. Use one for headlines (often a strong, bold font) and one for body text (a clean, highly readable font). Too many fonts creates visual chaos.

5. Convert Text to Outlines

Before submitting your final file (usually as a PDF), convert all text layers to **outlines** (or vector paths). This prevents the printer's computer from substituting a font it doesn't have installed, ensuring your text looks exactly as you intended. If you skip this step, the printer might substitute fonts, ruining your layout.

**Key Takeaway:** Following these five steps will drastically reduce revision time and guarantee your printed materials align with the professional quality standards Sammy Worx is known for.
